# O62: A discovery during a prompt edit's restoration ends it with "Refresh the conversation to continue"

- Found in: O60's investigation, 2026-11-22 (module split items 4 and 5)
- Owner step: owner
- Status: closed

## Item

After a committed prompt edit (a native-prefix Handoff), `usePromptEditOperation`'s `onSettled` waits until the edited view shows the successor, captures that view's operation scope, then restores the prompt through several IndexedDB reads; it throws `sessions.promptEdit.targetUnavailable` when the scope stops being current before the restoration ends. Every discovery withholds the locators while it runs (`SessionLocatorSynchronizingError`), so each view goes pending and comes back with a new operation scope. The page starts a discovery on every `session_succession_committed` frame (`useSessionSuccession`, even for a succession it has applied) and when a feed reopens. The Relay publishes that frame through an asynchronous chain, not before the commit's HTTP answer, so a frame that arrives after the page's own refresh settled, or a feed that reopens then, ends the restoration: the notice says "Handoff complete. Refresh the conversation to continue." and the prompt waits for Retry refresh. O60's test hit it when its own notification came late; its fixture now sends the frame as it commits. Possible fixes: the restoration waits for the view again (within its 15 s) when only the scope changed, or a committed frame for an operation the page has applied starts no discovery. Both change product behaviour, which the module split did not

## Status

closed (2026-11-22). The cause is in the succession rules: a notice of a Handoff the page had already confirmed and applied started a discovery that could find nothing new, and every discovery withholds the locators while it runs. `notificationNeedsDiscovery` in `agent-client/src/succession/settlement.ts` decides that such a notice starts none (`committedRefreshOf` confirms the operation at the role's applied and committed revision); a notice of an operation the page has not applied, of one two roles hold, or of a role without a checkpoint still discovers. Pinned by its vector table (7 rows) and by the App test "edits a real App managed Team window…", which now delivers the notice inside the restoration with fixture timing: the restoration's read of the prompt draft sends the notice, waits until the page has handled it, and holds the discovery it started (if any) until the page has drawn it. Without the rule the test failed 5 of 5 runs with the notice left at "Refresh the conversation to continue"; with it, 5 of 5 passed. Mutations: notices that always discover fail the App test and 2 rows; notices that never discover fail 5 rows and 3 feed tests of `useSessionSuccession.effects.test`, whose notices now carry their replay as the transport sends it. A feed that reopens during a restoration still discovers, by design
