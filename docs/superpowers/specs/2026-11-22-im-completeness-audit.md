# IM completeness audit (I5b)

Status: done on 2026-11-22, branch `feat/i5-im-groups`. Each row of "Organised as an IM product" in the [Orchard design](2026-11-20-orchard-im-experience-design.md) was checked end to end on desktop and at 390 x 844 (Chromium and WebKit). A gap is fixed, or recorded as a decision.

Component specs are in `packages/agent-remote-lab/e2e/components`; their projects are named in each spec's first line.

| Requirement | Status | Fix or decision | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Groups**: one concept, sectioned Chats and directory, the same group as tiles | Fixed | Chats and the directory are sectioned by group in one order; Window mode lists groups in that order and says "group" (分组). The default group, Ungrouped (未分组), holds every assistant still in the group of its own that New assistant made. It is listed last and is not stored, so it has no settings of its own. | `kernel/assistant-groups.test.ts` (vector tables); `BotRoster.test.tsx`; `assistant-groups.spec.ts` (desktop, phone, WebKit) |
| Group operations: create, rename, reorder, add, remove, move, mute, notifications | Fixed | All are in the directory's group and assistant menus, and the assistant actions are also on the card in Chats. A move is planned and checked locally first (merge out of a group of one, transfer otherwise), then sent through the Relay's transfer and merge. Protocol: group `muted` and `notifications` and the `section.settings` edit, plus `muted` on a chat. No Controller release follows. An empty group can be deleted. | `assistant-groups.test.ts`; `window-groups.test.ts`; `window-workspaces.test.ts` (Relay); `assistant-groups.spec.ts` |
| **Chats** ordered by activity | Works; decision | Chats are ordered within each group by the latest reply, with pinned chats first. Decision: a send moves its chat when the assistant answers, so the list does not jump while assistants work. | `assistant-groups.test.ts` "sections Chats by group…" (catches both ordering mutations of the removed `projectChats` test) |
| Unread and needs-you dots | Fixed | Needs you now has its own ringed dot, shown in place of the unread dot. Mute never quiets it. A muted chat's unread dot is grey and does not mark its group. Decision: the rail and tab-bar badge belong to the notifications and badges step. | `BotRoster.test.tsx` "marks needs you…" |
| Pin (star) and mute | Fixed | Pin to top, Unpin, Mute and Unmute are on the card and in the directory. A muted chat shows a bell. Mute silences both reminders. A group's notifications can be "needs you and finished" or "only when it needs you". | `assistant-groups.test.ts` reminders; `session-notifications.test.ts` "shows only the reminders its rule admits" |
| Draft kept per chat | Works | The draft is shown in the row and restored in the composer, and it is shared through the Relay. | `App.shared-drafts.test.tsx`, `useSharedDraft.test.tsx`, flow E12 |
| **Contacts and presence**: working, idle, resting, Host offline | Works; decision | The directory lists every assistant, including resting and hidden ones, by group. The presence words come from `assistantStatus`. Decision: idle says the latest reply or "Ready when you are", not a word of its own. | `assistant-status.test.ts`, `AssistantDirectory.test.tsx`, `navigation.spec.ts` |
| **Search** across chats and messages | Fixed (partial); decision | The palette finds an assistant by its name, group, label, session title, latest reply and draft. In Window mode it finds the assistant's session. The phone's Chats header now opens search. Decision: full-text history across every chat needs a Relay index, so history is searched inside a conversation. | `App.command-palette.test.tsx` "finds a chat by its latest reply…" |
| Search inside a conversation | Works | The conversation's search panel (Ctrl or ⌘+F) is in the top bar in Bot mode, on desktop and phone. | `TimelineSearch.test.tsx`, `session-search.browser.ts` |
| **Message actions**: copy | Fixed | Copy sits beside a sent message or a reply. With a mouse it shows on hover and focus; on a touch screen it shows in the swipe reveal. | `AgentTimeline.copy.test.tsx`, `timeline-time.spec.ts` |
| Quote | Works; decision | "Quote messages" quotes into another view in Window mode. Decision: an IM reply-quote in Chats is not built; its touch entry is the tile menu. | `quote-flow.test.ts`, inventory M9 |
| Resend after "Not sent" | Fixed | Retry and Delete are on the message. One wording, Not sent (没发出去), is now used in the timeline, the chips and the outbox. | `OutgoingMessageItem.test.tsx`, `OutboxMessages.test.tsx`, `message-delivery.spec.ts` |
| Retract or edit where the agent allows | Works; fixed; decision | Edit ("Edit from this message") follows each session's `send_message` capability. It now stays beside the message for a mouse at any window width. Decision: retract works only before a message leaves (chip ×, outbox Cancel). No agent protocol withdraws input the Host has accepted. | `SessionPromptEdit.test.tsx`, `timeline-time.spec.ts` "keeps Edit … narrow desktop window" |
| **Delivery states**: sending, sent, not delivered, check delivery | Works; decision | The states are sending, then Sent or Queued (waiting for the echo), then gone. A message is unconfirmed or Not sent until the send state machine settles it. Decision: "Check delivery" stays automatic. The client asks the Host when the session is ready again, at most three times, and the label tells the person to check. | `send-state.test.ts`, `remote-session-client.test.ts`, `message-delivery.spec.ts`, flows E4, E9 |
| **Typing and working indicator** | Works; decision | The live status line (typing dots) shows in the conversation, and "Working on it" shows in the row and top bar. Decision: the owner's typing on another device is not shown. Its draft appears there after about 800 ms. | inventory S1, S2; `App.bot-attention.test.tsx` |
| **Multi-device sync** of drafts and reads | Works | Drafts and read positions are shared through the Relay, and Chats rows follow both. | seams S3 "shared read positions", "shared drafts"; `session-reads.test.ts`; flow E12 |
| Notifications | Fixed; decision | Mute and per-group notifications apply in both modes. Notifications now speak the reader's language. Decision: phones get push in the notifications and badges step, since a touch screen has no in-page notifications. | `session-notifications.test.ts` |
