## 1. Inventory of the page-return listeners (Task 3)

Every `visibilitychange`, `pageshow`, `online`, `focus` and `watchPageResume` listener of the lab and the web package, and what S4b did with it.
The invariant: a list loader refetches on mount, on its own interval while the page is visible (`watchList`: five minutes unless the list moves faster) and when the
shared feed socket reopens (`watchFeedReopen`). Becoming visible refetches nothing and restarts the interval. `watchPageResume` keeps two jobs: the access check
(`GET /auth/status`, the first request) and the socket probe that finds a dead socket and reopens it, which triggers the reload of every list.

The control paints at 67 to 132 ms because it showed the old localStorage display cache before the access lease was confirmed; the privacy rule (no stored row before `GET /auth/status` confirms the lease) moves first content to after that answer, one round trip later. No slower than the control at p50 except within noise. Journal-served: 25 of 25 per condition.
- **Control finding.** The control's `open-fresh` stalled at "Opening session" for 30 s (messages 0) at 1x and 4x CPU, on a different session each time and again on a rerun; at 100 ms it ran 30 samples. S4b hit the same stall in a long single run (all four conditions) and not when each condition ran in its own process (25 of 25). Treated as an intermittent first-open stall that is not specific to S4b; its cause is not known.

## 3. Departures from the plan

- The store covers every session read through the replica, not only journal-served ones (the deleted localStorage cache did, and non-journal sessions would otherwise lose their preview).
- A failed lease check purges only when the Relay answers 401 or 403; a network failure hides the rows and keeps the records.
- The first-open shell of a conversation with no rows keeps its opening words (P2 owns them); only a conversation whose stored rows await the lease gets the quiet shell.
- Control ran for 5 samples per return condition, S4b for 10; `hidden-long` (frozen 300 s) was not run, and the dead-socket return (`hidden-dead`) was not run.
- Quota and eviction caps are the plan's starting values (32 MiB disk, 4 MiB preload); no measurement shows they need changing: the stored records of these sessions are a few KiB to tens of KiB.

**What changed and what was measured.**
- **T1 after the lease ended** (the cached lease's expiry set to the past before each reload, 30 samples per condition, and one reload after really waiting 125 s): 0 opening-label, 0 opening-word and 0 row-less states, content at mount; content since navigation is the same as with a live lease (75 ms at 1x, 119 ms at 4x CPU, 407 ms at 100 ms against 405 ms), because the page's first frames wait on its own loading and the Relay's answer comes within it. The rows then wait for `GET /auth/status`; the page shows nothing until they exist.
- **A feed that reopens while the page is hidden** marks lists due and refreshes them once when the page is shown. Live-socket returns are unchanged (1 to 2 requests, 0 states). A dead socket (`hidden-dead`) now makes **13 to 14 requests** in every condition: every list refreshes once on show. The budget applies to a live-socket return; a dead socket that needs its lists refreshed is legitimate.
- **The `open-fresh` stall persists at 1x for both arms** (control 2 of 2 attempts, S4b 2 of 2), and at 4x CPU and 100 ms the runs complete. With `about:blank` before the storage clear, and a dump on timeout, the page is stuck at "Opening session" with: `Too many control requests. Retrying automatically; no messages will be resent.`; repeated `429 Too Many Requests` on the Relay's control endpoints (the window operations and their polling: `GET windows/:id/changes` pending 10 s, `POST windows/:id/operations`, `GET windows/:id/operations/:id`, and `404` on the operation reads); one session-channel WebSocket open and ready, no timeline frame. It is the Relay's control-request rate limit: at 1x the runner clears storage and reopens a session faster than the budget refills, and each fresh open registers a new window. Not a missing frame or a client hang. No product code was changed for it (dumps in `.tmp/s4b/stall-*.json`).

**Deletions after checking every mode.**
- **Principal plumbing: deleted** (`principalId` in the key, record, index and gate, the stored lease's principal, the principal-change branch of `GatewayController`, the foreign purge in `preload`). Hosted, Cloudflare and Devbox all give the access a `basePath` of the form `/u/<64 hex>/` (`parseAccess` refuses any other), which holds the person; a different person is a different Relay URL, and the existing path-change branch retires and purges the old Relay's records. The local fixture (no gate) has one person. No mode lacks the person in the Relay URL. Tests: another Relay URL sees none of the rows; the lease is stored with its access.
- **One write rule: done.** The observer's 250 ms and 2 s timers and its per-session `pagehide` and `visibilitychange` listeners are gone: a record is saved when a forward or tail page is confirmed, when a turn ends and once when a connection learns its session; `DeviceStore` flushes what is queued on `pagehide` and when the page hides, one listener pair for the page.
- **Quiet shell: deleted** (`QuietShell`, `storedRowsPending`, `cachedRowsScope`, the composer prop). A conversation that is opening shows nothing until rows exist; the Host-offline notice and the draft-only composer keep their words.
- **HTTP read for an unsubscribed client: deleted.** The only caller was a test fixture that never connects; it now passes `timelineRecovery: 'http'`.
- **One stored-row path (`useStoredDisplay`, `heldWhileResolving`, `cachedSessionState`): not deleted.** A record names an agent id, but a view that is not the requested conversation resolves its agent through the attach, and in window mode the window document (a network read) names the conversation after first paint; both need a display that does not depend on a replica.
