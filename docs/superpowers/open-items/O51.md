## Item

Full smoke M1 (seed 70621): at 1,500 ms round trip with 2 % and 10 % loss the revisit painted its first rows 9,625 ms and 19,931 ms after the document, after the Relay's first page, not from the device store (they passed at 50 and 300 ms, and on 2026-11-18 at 1,500 ms)

## Status

closed (harness defect, not a product defect): the first violating event is the revisit's first row after the `timeline_page` of the session at 110,942 ms. The first open of that condition subscribed three sessions (the Window's panes); the opened session's tail page arrived at 99,524 ms and the probe closed the page 0.8 s later, because `waitForKeptRecord` counted any record of the device store, and the other panes' records were already written. So the revisit had no record of its own session to paint (clause: the device store paints only a kept record of the same session, S7). Run alone at 1,500 ms with and without loss, the revisit painted from the device store in 118 to 127 ms. The probe now waits for the record of the session it opened (`keptRecordOf`, pinned by `smoke/lib/probe.test.ts` with the store's own `deviceKey`); M1 re-run with the same seed passed, the 1,500 ms revisits painting from the device store in 109 and 119 ms
