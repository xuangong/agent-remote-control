# O30: On a phone profile the movable floating controls sit over the header controls

- Found in: M15
- Owner step: owner
- Status: closed

## Item

On a phone profile the movable floating controls (Window, Team, TPM) sit over the header controls: the Trace tab, the View options Header checkbox, 'Close Favorites' and the quote controls are not clickable ('subtree intercepts pointer events')

## Status

closed (15ad2fc6, 50d837bf): the default column starts below the app bar, the timeline toolbar and a full-screen Ask or TPM heading, and goes under an open View options, Favorites panel or TPM window (unit tests, component spec `floating-controls-clearance`, 7 phone tests un-skipped). The end gate found that the clearance compared layout-viewport bounds with a placement in the visible area, pushing the column 80 to 88 px too low under a keyboard pan; it is now measured in the visible area (unit test; `mobile-keyboard-controls` 30 passed on three projects).
