## 1. Inventory of the page-return listeners (Task 3)

Every `visibilitychange`, `pageshow`, `online`, `focus` and `watchPageResume` listener of the lab and the web package, and what S4b did with it.
The invariant: a list loader refetches on mount, on its own interval while the page is visible (`watchList`: five minutes unless the list moves faster) and when the
shared feed socket reopens (`watchFeedReopen`). Becoming visible refetches nothing and restarts the interval. `watchPageResume` keeps two jobs: the access check
(`GET /auth/status`, the first request) and the socket probe that finds a dead socket and reopens it, which triggers the reload of every list.

**Verdicts.**
- **Return budget: met with live sockets at 1x and 4x CPU** (2 requests, `GET /auth/status` and `POST session-reads`; the control and P0: 13 to 14). **Missed at 100 ms round trip** in some samples (up to 6: a re-attach, `hosts`, `session-relations`, `session/call`): at that latency the proxy-side socket did not survive the 30 s hide in every sample, so the return reattached. Not diagnosed further.
- **T1: missed.** Opening-label and row-less states are not 0 (2 to 3), and content since mount at 4x CPU is 207 ms p50 / 307 ms p95 against 100 ms. The control paints at 67 to 132 ms because it showed the old localStorage display cache before the access lease was confirmed; the privacy rule (no stored row before `GET /auth/status` confirms the lease) moves first content to after that answer, one round trip later. The quiet shell was added to hide the opening words until then, but the runner still sees 2 to 3 label states and row-less states; this was not resolved.
- **T2: median met at 4x CPU and 100 ms** (1,110 ms p50 / 1,428 ms p95; the control 1,107 / 1,173), **p50 target (500 ms) missed at 100 ms round trip in both arms** (the round trips of the page's own requests). 1x: p95 4,383 ms from one outlier. No slower than the control at p50 except within noise. Journal-served: 25 of 25 per condition.
- **Control finding.** The control's `open-fresh` stalled at "Opening session" for 30 s (messages 0) at 1x and 4x CPU, on a different session each time and again on a rerun; at 100 ms it ran 30 samples. S4b hit the same stall in a long single run (all four conditions) and not when each condition ran in its own process (25 of 25). Treated as an intermittent first-open stall that is not specific to S4b; its cause is not known.

## 3. Departures from the plan

- The store covers every session read through the replica, not only journal-served ones (the deleted localStorage cache did, and non-journal sessions would otherwise lose their preview).
- A failed lease check purges only when the Relay answers 401 or 403; a network failure hides the rows and keeps the records.
- The first-open shell of a conversation with no rows keeps its opening words (P2 owns them); only a conversation whose stored rows await the lease gets the quiet shell.
- Control ran for 5 samples per return condition, S4b for 10; `hidden-long` (frozen 300 s) was not run, and the dead-socket return (`hidden-dead`) was not run.
- Quota and eviction caps are the plan's starting values (32 MiB disk, 4 MiB preload); no measurement shows they need changing: the stored records of these sessions are a few KiB to tens of KiB.
