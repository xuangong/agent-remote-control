## 2. What the person gets

- **One kind of bot.** Every bot has a Relay record (stable id, name, label, picture, **mission**, **chief**) and the same tools:
  `create_bot` (temporary or standing), `message` (with `status` and up to 3 attached files), `list_bots`, `read_bot`,
  `download_attachment`, `update_mission`, `stop_bot`, `retire_bot`, work items and timers, and the room tools. A bot that runs
  other bots is their **chief**, and they are its **staff** (zh 主管 / 成员); a chief runs at most 12.
- **Messages** between bots are at most 300 characters, delivered between turns, at most once. Every `message` returns
  `delivered`, `held`, `duplicate`, `forbidden` or `unreachable`. A turn started by one message answers its sender with its final
  text when it sent no `message` back, so a bot on a provider without tools can still be asked. When the person writes to a
  standing bot whose chief is a bot, the chief is told in one framed line.
- **Code between machines** goes through a git repository both Hosts reach first, and only without one as a git bundle attached
  to a message; a received file is verified and never applied by itself.
- **Chains and the audit.** Each input of the person and each timer starts a chain that records the messages, bots, turns and
  tokens it set off. Settings → Audit lists chains and stops a chain or one bot's part of it, pauses, resumes and retires bots.
  Thresholds on tokens, depth and minutes send a "needs you" notification and never stop work. A page learns a passed chain without a push: a needs-you mark on the chain's first bot in Chats and a
  count on Settings → Assistants, until the audit has shown the chain. A staff bot's needs-you push collapses under its chief's
  notification ("Chief: Reviewer needs you"), and the chief's row shows the mark while its staff needs the person.
- **The Workbench.** Chats groups bots under their chief with a status line and a work line; messages between bots fold into
  one line; a temporary bot sits beside its chief with Send, Interrupt, Queue, Stop and Keep; details show the mission, staff and
  work; rooms show in Bot mode.
- **The folds.** `/side <text>` starts a temporary bot of the bot on screen. Call For Support, ordinary Teams (the Teams panel, its
  inbox tools and file transfers) and TPM (its workspace, intents, todo, heartbeat and background reviews) are gone.

- **Step 8** ([plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-24-bots-8-mentions-plan.md)). Each bot keeps a board: a plan note whose changes
  carry a why, and items with an assignee, status, links, the brief as sent, the latest report and a one-line result. Work items are
  gone. The main session coordinates and reads its board first. A temporary bot fills in the item it was created for. A standing
  staff bot's report only sets the item's latest report; its chief sets status and result. Whoever is assigned an item is told when it
  changes, and the bot itself is told when the person edits its items or plan note. The person reads, creates and edits items in a
  bot's details, and opens the bot doing each one.
- **Mentions.** `@Name` in any conversation asks that bot. Its answer session (one per bot and conversation, retired after an hour
  idle) answers in place. The conversation's session and the bot's main session receive nothing. Messages from bots always reach the
  main session.
- **Memory and chiefs.** Each bot keeps a memory of at most 50 short facts beside its mission, written by its own sessions and the
  person. `set_chief` moves a standing bot, telling the old chief, the new chief and the bot. A chief manages at most 12 bots, standing
  and temporary alike.

## 5. Deployment

Deploy the tip of `feat/bots-8-boards` as one release; the stack below it is not deployed on its own.

1. Relay first. It drops the old data above when it loads. Forward only: an older Relay cannot read the new state.
2. Then every Controller. There is no transition layer: an older Controller that sends a removed call (a Team file transfer, an
   ordinary Team action, a TPM path) has its uplink closed as invalid, so update every Controller right after the Relay.
3. Merge into `main`, then on `main` before pushing: `pnpm postmerge` (compatibility digest, open-items index, legacy token
   budgets, committed alone and checked).

## 6. Known limits

- Claude Code ignores instructions appended on resume, so standing instructions reach a bot as a message (design 5.1). A resumed
  Claude Code session keeps the schema of a tool it loaded before the tool changed.
- `read_bot` reads any standing bot of the account (needed by the pointer a cut final-text reply carries); the design's
  narrower rule (chiefs and the person only) and `peers` are not built.
- Attachments show in the page only as lines in the message body; uploads are not resumable.
- The shared-repository and bundle paths ran live on one Host standing in for two machines; two real machines were not used.
- A temporary bot's inputs are still framed `[Task …]`; the page shows them as task cards.
- Room and bot timers run on the Relay's in-process timers; a hosted Relay arms them again when it loads the account.
- Coverage removed with deleted code and not replaced: the attribution layout component spec and App-level attribution navigation.
- Intermittent, not from this work: E12's slow access check (`e12-sign-in.e2e.ts`) timed out once in a WebKit run and passed 5
  of 5 twice alone; the three full runs since passed it.
- A page left open on Settings still counts the conversation it last showed as on screen, so no push goes out for that bot
  (the existing rule of push reminders); the page's own mark and count tell the person instead, and the first E15b flow closes the page
  to see the push.

## 8. Step 9: the old ways of working, done with bots

The owner asked to prove the fold by doing what Side, Call For Support, Team and TPM were used for with real bots, recording it
and reflecting, rather than writing tests. Four records, each run once (run-to-run variance applies):
[first](../superpowers/reviews/2026-11-24-old-ways-with-bots.md),
[second](../superpowers/reviews/2026-11-25-old-ways-with-bots-after-fixes.md),
[third](../superpowers/reviews/2026-11-25-old-ways-with-bots-third.md),
[fourth](../superpowers/reviews/2026-11-25-old-ways-with-bots-fourth.md). What they changed
([plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-11-25-bots-9-review-plan.md)):

- A `/side` answers beside its conversation: a card with the side's full final text and Bring back; nothing reaches the main bot.
  A side costs 26k to 55k tokens and 1 to 2 model calls (first record 56k to 63k; second and third 81k to 143k).
- Delegation is one path. Asking a bot for help is handing it work by message; no help-specific feature (owner, 2026-11-25). Base
  instructions v10: ask the bot that knows instead of guessing (copying a neighbour's style is a guess); do not start unasked work;
  chiefs read their board instead of asking staff for progress; answer a message with `message` and `reply_to`. In the fourth
  record the builder asked the bot that knows by itself in both variants (0 of 2 in the second and third).
- A message never reassigns an item; assignment is by `create_bot` with an item or `update_board`; notices and a new bot's first
  input carry the item and its brief; an assignee may name its chief's item in a message.
- Final text answers a message only for a session that was not given the bot tools; bots with tools answer with `message`. This
  removed the loop of closing monologues routed as answers.
- One approval per chain and folder covers every tool kind (owner, 2026-11-25): the Team's approvals went from 37 to 1–2.
- An `ack` waits for the next input instead of starting a turn. Its receipt once froze seats `busy` (N-7, fixed in `83d45904`
  with a real-broker test); three Team runs after the fix kept every seat moving.
- Found and fixed on the way: an answer to a request lost when the request was itself a reply; the composer draft kept on the
  Relay after a side; Codex history pages that came back empty for the newest entry (`bc984a6b`).
