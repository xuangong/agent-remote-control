# O67: The WebKit composer-owner component test loses its own selection now and then

- Found in: the I5a end gate, `pnpm test:components` at `5e924da3` (2026-11-22)
- Owner step: owner
- Status: closed

## Item

`[webkit-desktop] composer-owner.spec.ts` > "keeps the editor, its focus and its selection when the session behind the draft owner moves from A to B" failed once in the full component tier: the selection the test sets with `addRange` read back empty (`anchor 0, focus 0`) at line 17, before the fixture moves the session, so before any product code runs on it. Run alone it passed 10 of 11 times (2 of 3, then 8 of 8 with `--repeat-each=8`), and it passed in the full pre-check run of the same day. Its fixture renders `AgentComposer` on a stub state; I5a changed nothing in that import graph except a drawer rule of `app.css` that the fixture does not render. Not checked on the base commit. A likely cause is WebKit taking the focus or the selection back from the page between `focus()` and `addRange`.

## Cause

The test raced ProseMirror's own focus follow-up, not the product. `EditorView` answers a `focus` event with a 20 ms timer (`prosemirror-view`, `handlers.focus`): if the page selection differs from the one its DOM observer last read, it writes its own selection (here the caret at 0) over the page. The test called `focus()` and then set the range with `addRange` a Playwright round trip later; when that round trip is shorter than 20 ms, the timer fires after `addRange` and before ProseMirror has read it. Trace under load (8 busy loops) of a failing run: `focusin 329`, `selectionchange 329 (0,0)`, `addRange 348`, timer fires at `349` with the page at `(2,9)` and leaves it at `(0,0)`. Forced deterministically by holding back `selectionchange` for 100 ms after `focus()` and `addRange`: the page ends at `(0,0)` every time. The product replaces nothing unless the page selection was set by script inside that window; a person's selection reaches ProseMirror through its own events, so no product change.

## Fix

The test waits after `focus()` on a 30 ms page timer, which runs after ProseMirror's 20 ms one, before it sets the selection (`composer-owner.spec.ts`). Waiting for `selectionchange` was tried first and was not enough (2 of 90 runs failed): ProseMirror reads that event later than it is dispatched.

Proof, `webkit-desktop`, `--repeat-each`, 8 busy loops beside the run: the `selectionchange` variant failed 2 of about 90; the timer variant passed 100 of 100.
