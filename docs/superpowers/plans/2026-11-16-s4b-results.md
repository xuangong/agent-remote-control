## 1. Inventory of the page-return listeners (Task 3)

Every `visibilitychange`, `pageshow`, `online`, `focus` and `watchPageResume` listener of the lab and the web package, and what S4b did with it.
The invariant: a list loader refetches on mount, on its own interval while the page is visible (`watchList`: five minutes unless the list moves faster) and when the
shared feed socket reopens (`watchFeedReopen`). Becoming visible refetches nothing and restarts the interval. `watchPageResume` keeps two jobs: the access check
(`GET /auth/status`, the first request) and the socket probe that finds a dead socket and reopens it, which triggers the reload of every list.
