# O66: Bot mode's panel widths are applied over the chats module's stylesheet, and Window mode's sidebar keeps its own handle

- Found in: I5a, desktop panels (2026-11-22), which could not edit the chats and windows feature modules while the IM audit worked in them
- Owner step: the next change to `workbench-chats` or `workbench-windows`
- Status: open

## Item

The frame owns the panel widths (`src/kernel/panel-layout.ts`, `src/shell/DesktopFrame.tsx`). Two places still keep their own:
- **Bot mode's grid.** `features/chats/bot-workspace.css` gives the assistant list and the details fixed columns (260 and 300 px). The frame's `src/shell/panels.css` overrides them with `--lab-panel-sidebar` and `--lab-panel-details` through more specific selectors (`.lab-frame > .lab-bot-workspace…`). A later change to those columns in `bot-workspace.css` has no effect while the override stands. Fix: let `bot-workspace.css` read the two lengths (`var(--lab-panel-sidebar, 260px)`, `var(--lab-panel-details, 300px)`) and delete the two rules from `panels.css`; the component spec `desktop-panels.spec.ts` checks the result.
- **Window mode's sidebar.** It keeps its own handle and stored width (`features/windows/SidebarResize.tsx`, `agent-remote.sidebar-width`, 220 to 360 px), whose maximum assumes the 60 px rail. The frame's rail gives way so Window mode's stage keeps its minimum (`layoutPanels`, `mode: 'window'`), but the two handles do not share one rule. Fix: move Window mode's sidebar onto `layoutPanels` and `PanelHandle`, then delete `SidebarResize`.
