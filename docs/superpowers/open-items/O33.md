## Item

A bot view whose session is missing, opened over from the directory, now opens a new primary beside it; the old spec expected the view to be rebound

## Status

closed: not a regression and not a defect. The baseline tag does the same (the restored spec step fails there identically: a third view, a new primary, is created and `main` keeps its binding). Root cause, instrumented: the Window keeps the missing session's binding, so `isBotView` holds, every group has the Bot appearance by default, and `bindPlan` answers `open-beside` (rule 6); the bot design's stage 2b lists 'a new session' among the opens that land beside a bot. The removed step predated rule 6. Pinned by the restored step in `window-workspaces.spec.ts` with the intended outcome (a new primary is selected, the missing view keeps its reference and note; a mutation of `bindPlan` to bind a primary bot in place fails it) and the `session-binding` unit case. Owner question left: whether a bot whose session the Host reports missing should take another session in place (it needs a missing-session fact in `bindPlan`, and rule 1 of the Relay refuses that rebind in a group with bot details)
