## 2. What the deployment gives the person

- Every bot has a Relay record with a stable id, its name, label, picture and **mission**; groups keep only where it sits. Existing
  bots move into records when the Relay loads its state, with ids derived from account, Window and view.
- The mission is written in the bot's details.
- Every bot has the task, message and room tools (no coordinator or plain mode); any bot can message any other.
- Every bot has an **inbox**: a Team of its main session alone. Messages from other bots and its **standing instructions** (base
  instructions v1 and its mission) arrive there between its turns, at most once. A bot with a mission gets them when they change; a
  bot without one gets them with the first message another bot sends it. A deployment therefore wakes no bot by itself, except bots
  whose mission someone wrote.
- The page folds standing instructions and messages between bots into quiet lines.

## 4. Deployment

1. Relay first: it migrates bot records on load. Forward only: an older Relay cannot read a state with `bot` records.
2. Then every Controller: a Controller older than the stack sees no inboxes and gets no bot tools; nothing breaks for it.
3. Before pushing, from `main` after the merge: `pnpm postmerge` (compatibility digest, open-items index, budgets).

## 5. Known limits, after the first deployment

- Claude Code ignores instructions appended on resume, so standing instructions reach a bot as a message (design 5.1).
- A bot on a provider without tools (OpenCode, DSH) receives messages but cannot answer them: replies from final text (design 3.3)
  are not built.
- Tasks keep their own tools (`create_task`, `report_task`, …); `create_bot`, the `message` tool with `status`, temporary bots,
  work items, timers and the audit follow the plan's later steps.
- Call For Support, Team, Side and TPM are unchanged until their folds (plan step 7).
