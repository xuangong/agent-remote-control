# O71: Rooms live in the Teams panel and are opened only by an assistant

- Found in: M4 of the [assistant tasks plan](../plans/2026-11-23-assistant-tasks-plan.md) (2026-11-23)
- Owner step: step 3 of the [bots plan](../plans/2026-11-23-fold-into-assistants-plan.md)
- Status: open

## Item

Rooms run on the Team path (`agent-relay-core/src/rooms.ts`): an assistant opens one with `open_room`, the Relay asks one member at a
time with the messages it has not seen, members answer with `room_say` or `room_pass`, and the person reads, writes and ends a room
in the Teams panel. Still missing:

- **Opening from the page.** The person cannot open a room or add a member; they ask an assistant to.
- **Where a room shows.** A room is a row of the Teams panel, not a conversation in Bot mode's list, and its members' turns are folded
  lines in their own conversations. A room's result is not announced in its opener's conversation.
- **Scheduling.** Done on `feat/bots-3-rooms-work`: a Relay timer closes a room at its deadline and moves a turn on when its member did
  not answer, with nobody acting; members answer, pass and end a room with `message`. On Cloudflare the timer lives while the Relay
  object is awake; an object that slept moves the room at its next activity. The transcript keeps the latest 100 messages and a room
  at most 24 turns.

Fix: rooms as message targets in Bot mode, closed on time by a Relay timer ([bots design](../specs/2026-11-23-bots-design.md) 4).
