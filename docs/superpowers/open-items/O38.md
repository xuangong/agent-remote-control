# O38: The 30-minute M8 soak fails `memory-flat`

- Found in: sweep end gate
- Owner step: owner
- Status: closed

## Item

The 30-minute M8 soak fails `memory-flat`: the page heap after a collection rose from 12.8 MB (first sample; first-quarter median 18.0 MB) to 38.1 MB (last-quarter median 35.8 MB, limit 32.3 MB) while the three panes grew by 1,086 rows; extra rows and sockets stayed flat. The 10-minute run at the open-items sweep grew 12.7 to 24.3 MB for about 360 rows, so the growth per row is not larger than before this sweep, but the invariant cannot tell a leak from retained conversation: it compares a growing page with a fixed 1.5 times plus 5 MB

## Status

closed (final slice, 2026-11-19): no leak. The invariant now judges the heap per retained row: samples carry the rendered rows, and after the soak a fresh page opens the same Window and loads older history until it holds the soaked page's rows; the soaked heap may be at most 1.25 times the fresh page's heap per row plus 5 MiB, and growing rows with no fresh page are not judged (3 invariant tests). 30-minute run, seed 8210: 1,086 rows; soaked page 38.1 MB (35.1 KB per row), fresh page holding the same 1,086 rows 36.3 MB (33.4 KB per row), so 1.85 MB (5 percent) after 180 cycles is all the soak adds. M8 passed. The page keeps every row of an open conversation at about 33 KB each (10,000 rows would be about 330 MB); bounding retained rows is a product decision, not a leak
